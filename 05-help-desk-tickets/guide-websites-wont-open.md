# Websites won't open: quick checks before you call IT

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Work.** A guide written for the fictional Corestack office. It is based on what I learned in [Project 02](../02-network-troubleshooting/README.md).

**Use this when** websites will not open on your computer. It takes about five minutes and helps IT fix it faster.

## Step 1: Try more than one site
Open two or three different websites. If only one site fails, the problem may be with that site, not with your computer.

## Step 2: Check you are connected
Look at the Wi-Fi icon at the bottom right of your screen. If it shows no connection or a warning, click it and reconnect to the office Wi-Fi.

## Step 3: Read the message on the page
Write down exactly what the page says. These are common messages:
- **"This site can't be reached" or "could not find host":** your computer could not find the website's address.
- **"Refused to connect":** the address was found but the connection was turned down. On a work computer this can mean the site is blocked on purpose.

## Step 4: Try another browser
Open a different browser, such as Edge or Chrome, and try the same site. If it works there, tell IT which browser fails.

## Step 5: Restart your computer
Restart it once and try again. This fixes many connection problems.

## Step 6: Optional quick test, if you are comfortable
1. Press the Windows key, type `cmd`, and press Enter.
2. Type `ping 8.8.8.8` and press Enter.
3. Type `ping google.com` and press Enter.

Tell IT what each one says:
- Replies for both mean your connection works.
- Replies for the first but an error for the second can mean a problem with name lookup (DNS).
- An error for both means your computer is not reaching the internet at all.

## When to call IT, and what to tell them
Call IT if the sites still do not open. Tell them:
- Which sites fail, and which work.
- The exact message you see.
- Which browsers you tried.
- Whether Wi-Fi shows connected.
- Whether anything changed just before it started, such as an update or a new setting.

**Please do not** change network settings you do not understand, and never share your password with anyone, including IT.
